# IDEALX SMSF campaign: copy v1

This carries the LibertyStone concepts over to IDEALX: the hero spot "One place" and the five comedy concepts from `reports/SMSF crypto ads Australia.md`. Every product claim below is taken from idealx.com as published on 15 October 2026. Lines that need a feature confirmed first are marked **confirm**.

Conventions from `brand/BRAND.md`:
- Short and plain, with no exclamation marks and no emoji.
- Each headline has one accent word, set in Instrument Serif with an indigo underline, shown here as *word*.
- The site says "digital assets", not "crypto". The characters can say crypto; the brand voice says digital assets.

## Campaign line and running device

- **Master line:** Where your SMSF *lives*. (the site's own footer line)
- **Supporting:** One home. One door. Every room.
- **Series device:** "Trustee Rule No. X" supers. Each rule is a character: the Deed, the Bouncer, the Auditor, the Clock and Gary.
- **Payoff claims we can use, as published:**
  - "Everything the entity holds, side by side."
  - "A wallet that belongs to the entity."
  - "Live, not quarterly."
  - "Statements your accountant can use as they arrive."
  - "Adviser, accountant and administrator work in the same record, each with the key you gave them and nothing more."
  - "Each member, trustee or director is invited to complete their own identity check, on their own device."
  - "Held by custodians and partners, not by us."

## 1. The Reading of the Deed (30 s hero)

- **Script:**
  - The lawyer reads: "The trustees may invest in shares... property... and cash."
  - Pause.
  - Trustee: "And the crypto?"
  - The lawyer turns the page, turns it back, then holds it up to the light.
- **Super:** Trustee Rule No. 1: your deed has to allow it.
- **VO over the payoff:** When your deed and strategy cover it, IDEALX keeps shares, ETFs and digital assets side by side, with a wallet that belongs to the fund.
- **End super:** Read the *deed* first.
- **Social primary text:** Plenty of trust deeds were written for shares, property and cash. If yours now covers digital assets, IDEALX holds them beside everything else your fund owns.
- **YouTube version:** the punchline asset becomes "ETFs" and digital assets come out of the VO.

## 2. Not On the List (15 s social)

- **Script:**
  - The scene: a velvet rope, "The Smith Family Super Fund", a bouncer holding a clipboard marked "SIS Act".
  - A coin in sunglasses, "Dad's personal bitcoin", tries to get in.
  - Bouncer: "Related party. Not tonight, mate."
  - Coin: "But I'm family."
  - Bouncer: "Exactly."
- **Super:** Trustee Rule No. 2: the fund buys its own, with fund money.
- **End super:** A wallet that belongs to the *fund*.
- **Social primary text:** Your personal crypto can't move into your SMSF. The fund buys its own, with fund money, and IDEALX gives it a wallet of its own.
- **Platforms:** Meta and TikTok only. There is no shares-only version.

## 3. Show and Tell (30 s hero, or two 15 s cutdowns: "The Screenshot" and "The Sock")

- **Script:**
  - Trustee one holds up a printed screenshot. The auditor stamps it NOT SUFFICIENT.
  - Trustee two opens a shoebox with a hardware wallet in a sock: "It's definitely the fund's. It's got a sticker."
  - Auditor: "Can I see inside?" The trustee hugs the box.
  - Trustee three hands over an IDEALX statement and activity view. The auditor nods and reaches for a calculator.
- **Super:** Trustee Rule No. 3: a screenshot isn't evidence.
- **End super:** Statements your accountant can use as they *arrive*.
- **Social headline:** Show your *working*.
- **Social primary text:** Auditors want more than a screenshot. IDEALX traces every movement in plain language, with statements your accountant can use as they arrive.

There is no "audit-ready" or "audit done" anywhere. The calculator stays in shot.

## 4. The Thirtieth of June (20 to 30 s, May to July flight)

- **Script:**
  - 11:58 pm. Three monitors are open, with a sticky note: "same exchange as last year??"
  - Accountant, on the phone: "I need the closing price. From the same place as last year."
  - Midnight strikes, and a CSV prints like a receipt and runs out the door.
  - A year later the same trustee is asleep at 11:58 pm.
- **Super:** Trustee Rule No. 4: value it at 30 June.
- **VO over the payoff:** Your accountant works in the same record you see, with the key you gave them and nothing more.
- **End super:** Live, not *quarterly*.
- **Social headline:** Not another *midnight*.
- **Social primary text:** Every 30 June your fund's holdings need a market value. With IDEALX, your accountant works in the same record you do, live, not quarterly.
- **Confirm:** a 30 June valuation feature. The site doesn't mention one, so the copy doesn't claim it. If it exists, the payoff can say so.

## 5. Only Gary Knows (15 s social)

- **Script:**
  - Formal fund minutes at a backyard barbecue, with a gavel.
  - "Item four: where is the seed phrase?"
  - Both trustees turn slowly toward Gary, waving from a tinnie far out with no reception.
- **Super:** Trustee Rule No. 5: don't leave it all with Gary.
- **End super:** Every trustee, on their own *device*.
- **Social headline:** Gary has the *keys*.
- **Social primary text:** A fund meeting shouldn't wait for one trustee to get back in range. On IDEALX, each trustee completes their own identity check, on their own device.
- **Confirm:** that every trustee gets their own access to the fund's wallet. Don't imply that custody risk goes away.
