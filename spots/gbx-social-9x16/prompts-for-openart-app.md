# GBX 9:16 social pieces: prompts to paste into Flux 3 video in the OpenArt app

Settings for every clip: text to video, aspect 9:16, largest portrait size offered, audio off, 6 to 8 s (D1: 5 to 6 s).

## How Flux wants to be prompted (from the prompt-optimizer skill)

- Descriptive sentences, never keyword lists; Flux reads the prompt with a vision-language model.
- Word order matters: the main subject goes first and gets the most attention.
- Under 80 words for a standard shot; elements past that are progressively ignored.
- Name a camera body and lens (Shot on Sony A7IV, 35mm lens) for photorealism; it is the highest-impact line.
- One strong lighting description per prompt; vague lighting gives vague results.
- No negative prompt support, so say what is there (blank page, screens dark, plain cups), never what is not.
- Quality tags (4k, masterpiece) and weight syntax (word:1.4) do nothing; drop them.
- Present tense, one camera move per shot, and the action written with its endpoint.

Treat each prompt as a starting point: keep the subject and action, and tune the lens, light and framing lines using the guide.
